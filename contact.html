03 / QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Eight questions we hear most often. Expand any item for the full answer.

What does ALATA actually do that an agency does not?

We operate as an embedded operating partner, not a campaign agency. That means we own the end-to-end P&L levers: catalogue integrity, live commerce scheduling, fulfilment SLA management, customer service quality, and financial reconciliation. You get a single dashboard (ALATA OS) with real-time contribution margin by channel, not vanity metrics. We staff local operators in each market — not freelancers — and we integrate directly with platform APIs for TikTok Shop, Shopee, and Lazada.

Which markets and platforms are live today?

As of Q1 2026 we operate in Singapore, Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Thailand, and Vietnam. Platform coverage: TikTok Shop (all six markets), Shopee (all six), Lazada (all six), Tokopedia (Indonesia), Tiki (Vietnam). We also support cross-border flows from China and global D2C sites into Southeast Asia via bonded warehousing and local entity setup.

How is pricing structured?

We do not publish a rate card because scope varies materially by GMV, SKU count, live hours, and fulfilment complexity. Typical engagements combine a monthly retainer (covering dedicated pod: strategist, ops lead, CS lead, data analyst) plus a performance component tied to contribution margin improvement. Minimum engagement is three months. We share a detailed scope and pricing worksheet after the discovery call.

What does onboarding look like and how long does it take?

Standard onboarding is 4–6 weeks: Week 1–2 (audit & access provisioning), Week 2–3 (catalogue migration & QA), Week 3–4 (fulfilment & CS SOP deployment), Week 4–5 (live commerce pilot & ad account structure), Week 5–6 (ALATA OS configuration & handover). Complex multi-market launches with entity incorporation can extend to 8–10 weeks. We provide a fixed Gantt with milestones and owners before signing.

Do you guarantee GMV or ROAS targets?

No. Any partner promising guaranteed GMV or ROAS is misrepresenting platform dynamics. We commit to operational SLAs: catalogue accuracy >99.8%, fulfilment on-time rate >98%, CS first-response <2 hours, financial reconciliation variance <0.5%. We model illustrative targets in proposals — labelled Illustrative targets — based on category benchmarks and your historical data. Performance clauses can be structured around contribution margin improvement, not top-line revenue.

How does ALATA OS differ from platform dashboards?

Platform dashboards show channel-level metrics in isolation. ALATA OS normalises data across TikTok Shop, Shopee, Lazada (and Tokopedia/Tiki) into a single schema: unified SKU master, attributable marketing spend, true landed cost per order, contribution margin per SKU per channel, live commerce attribution, and cash-flow forecasting. It includes workflow automation: auto-replenishment triggers, SLA breach alerts, creator commission calculations, and tax-compliant settlement reports. API-first, SOC 2 Type II pending.

What if we already have an in-house team or agency?

We plug in where leverage is highest. Common models: (a) We run live commerce and affiliate programmes while your team owns brand marketing; (b) We handle fulfilment, CS, and finance ops while you keep catalogue and ads; (c) We provide ALATA OS as a SaaS layer for your team to operate on. We sign clean data-sharing addenda and define RACI matrices upfront. No territorial conflicts — we are infrastructure, not ego.

How do we start a pilot?

Submit the form above with as much context as possible. We schedule a 45-minute discovery call (technical + commercial). Pilot fee is credited against the first retainer month if you continue. Decision gate at week 3 with a written readout.
