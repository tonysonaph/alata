Privacy, handled with care.

Policy details

A clear starting point.

This policy applies to ALATA Pte. Ltd., a Singapore-headquartered company, and to personal data processed through this website, our business communications and the services we provide to sellers, brands and other customers across Southeast Asia.

It also references the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 of Singapore (“PDPA”) and the Personal Data Protection Regulations 2021. Where a platform or service provider is acting as a data intermediary or service provider, the relevant contractual obligations will govern the parties’ respective responsibilities.

1. Who we are

ALATA Pte. Ltd. (“ALATA”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a Singapore-headquartered e-commerce enabler. We help sellers and brands prepare and operate their stores, manage listings and campaigns, support live commerce, coordinate fulfilment and customer service, and understand performance across marketplaces such as TikTok Shop, Shopee and Lazada.

For privacy enquiries, contact us at hello@alata.example or write to us at [Office address], Singapore [Postal code].

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to personal data that ALATA collects or processes in connection with:

  • visiting, browsing and using this website;
  • submitting enquiries, requesting information or speaking with our team;
  • applying for employment, contractors or work experience opportunities;
  • entering into a client, seller, brand, partner or supplier relationship with us;
  • providing e-commerce enablement, operational and advisory services;
  • receiving, creating, delivering or supporting customer orders;
  • administering accounts, invoices, payments, contracts and business records; and
  • operating our communications, security, analytics and business continuity processes.

Some marketplace platforms operate their own privacy policies and may be responsible for processing personal data collected through their own services. We recommend reviewing the relevant marketplace notice before submitting personal data to a platform.

3. Personal data and the PDPA

Under the PDPA, “personal data” means data relating to an individual who is identifiable from that data, or from that data and other information to which we have or are likely to have access. Depending on the circumstances in which it is collected, personal data may include:

  • name, title, professional role and contact details;
  • business email address, telephone number and postal or business address;
  • company, employer, brand, seller account and marketplace identifiers;
  • invoice, payment, banking, tax and accounting information;
  • order, delivery, returns, customer support and service interaction records;
  • website usage information, such as pages viewed, referring pages, device information and approximate location derived from an IP address;
  • communications with us, including enquiries, meeting notes, feedback and correspondence;
  • recruitment information, including CVs, application forms, interview records, right-to-work information and employment or engagement documents; and
  • photographic, video or other content submitted by an individual where necessary for a legitimate business purpose.

Personal data may include information provided directly by an individual, collected through a marketplace or another service provider, generated when we provide services, or observed through the use of our website and systems. We do not seek to collect sensitive personal data unless it is necessary, appropriate and lawfully permitted.

4. How we collect personal data

We collect personal data through several channels, including:

  • Directly from you. When you complete a contact form, speak with a representative, attend a meeting, submit documents or enter into an agreement with us.
  • From our clients and service providers. Where a client, brand, marketplace, logistics provider, payment provider or other partner provides information required to deliver agreed services, fulfil orders or meet legal obligations.
  • Through website interactions. Through cookies, local storage, server logs and similar technologies that help us operate, secure and understand use of the website.
  • From publicly available sources. Where appropriate for a business purpose, such as professional information relevant to a business enquiry, while respecting applicable law and the rights of the person concerned.
  • During recruitment. Through job applications, interviews, assessments, referrals, background screening conducted with appropriate consent, and our recruitment systems.

Where we rely on consent to process personal data, we will ask for that consent clearly and provide information that allows an individual to understand what they are agreeing to. Consent may be withdrawn at any time, subject to applicable law. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

5. Why we use personal data

We process personal data for the following purposes, as applicable:

  • responding to enquiries and providing requested information;
  • preparing proposals, scoping services and entering into or administering client arrangements;
  • providing store setup, listing, live commerce, advertising, fulfilment, logistics, customer service, analytics, finance and reconciliation services;
  • operating, maintaining, testing and improving this website and our services;
  • monitoring security, preventing fraud, investigating misuse and enforcing our terms;
  • managing payments, invoices, tax records, refunds and other financial administration;
  • meeting legal, regulatory, contractual and professional obligations;
  • recruitment, talent assessment, workforce administration and applicant communication;
  • sending relevant service, business or recruitment communications where permitted;
  • analysing website performance and understanding general user behaviour;
  • establishing, exercising or defending legal claims; and
  • creating and publishing editorial or marketing content where an individual has agreed to participate.

Where we process personal data to perform a contract with a client or an individual, the legal basis may be the performance of that contract. Where we rely on legitimate interests, we do so only where the processing is proportionate and we have considered the impact on the individual. In other circumstances, we may rely on consent, legal obligation, vital interests or another basis recognised under applicable law.

6. How we use and share personal data

Personal data may be shared with carefully selected categories of recipients where necessary to deliver our services or meet legal obligations, including:

  • platforms and marketplaces used to operate seller accounts, publish content, accept orders and manage customer interactions;
  • service providers supporting hosting, cloud infrastructure, communications, customer support, analytics, security, payments and professional advice;
  • freight, delivery, warehousing, returns and fulfilment partners;
  • payment providers, financial institutions, auditors, insurers and professional advisers;
  • business partners, contractors and prospective or actual clients where an agreed service requires it;
  • authorities, regulators, law enforcement agencies or other parties where disclosure is required or permitted by law;
  • professional networks, recruitment providers and reference-check providers for recruitment purposes; and
  • a buyer, seller, investor or successor in connection with a corporate reorganisation, financing or sale, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal data. We do not disclose personal data for another party’s independent marketing without an appropriate lawful basis and any notice or consent required by law.

7. International data transfers

ALATA is headquartered in Singapore, and the services we support may involve personal data being processed in other countries, including where marketplace, hosting, customer service, logistics or technology providers operate. When personal data is transferred outside Singapore, we will take appropriate steps to protect it in accordance with applicable law.

Such steps may include contractual protections, access controls, encryption in transit and at rest, security assessments, data minimisation, limited access and other technical and organisational measures. The final policy should identify the relevant jurisdictions and transfer mechanisms once ALATA’s actual supplier and operating arrangements have been confirmed.

8. Retention and disposal

We retain personal data only for as long as it is reasonably necessary and proportionate for the purpose for which it was collected, including to provide contracted services, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and meet legal, tax, accounting or regulatory requirements.

Retention periods may vary according to the type of information, the relationship with the individual and the legal or operational need concerned. Examples of potential retention triggers include the duration of a client relationship, the completion of an order or project, unresolved complaints, the limitation period for legal claims, and statutory record-keeping periods.

When retention is no longer required, we delete or anonymise personal data, or take steps to ensure it can no longer be identified, depending on the applicable circumstances and legal requirements.

9. Data security

We recognise the importance of protecting personal data and apply reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature and sensitivity of the information. These may include:

  • role-based access and the principle of least privilege;
  • secure authentication and multifactor authentication where appropriate;
  • encryption of data in transit and at rest;
  • secure development and change management practices;
  • logging, monitoring and controlled administrative procedures;
  • regular risk assessment, vulnerability management and business continuity planning;
  • vendor review and contractual security obligations; and
  • employee training and confidentiality obligations.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We work to protect personal data but cannot guarantee absolute security. Individuals should take care when sharing sensitive information with us and use appropriate security for their own systems.

10. Cookies and website technologies

This website may use essential cookies or similar technologies required for core operation, security, accessibility and the saving of preferences. Where permitted, analytics or other non-essential technologies may help us understand aggregate website use and improve the experience.

Browser settings can usually be used to block or remove cookies. However, disabling certain technologies may affect some website features. A separate cookie notice or consent mechanism should be added before deploying non-essential analytics or marketing technologies, where required.

11. Our lawful responsibilities under the PDPA

As a data intermediary, ALATA may be required to process personal data only in accordance with the instructions of a client, an applicable written agreement and the PDPA. We would not process such data for our own purposes except where permitted under the PDPA, for example where necessary to provide a contracted service, comply with a legal obligation, or carry out a permitted matching or use activity.

Where we act as a data intermediary, the relevant client remains responsible for decisions concerning the purposes and means of processing its personal data, while ALATA remains responsible for performing the processing services securely and according to the applicable agreement.

Where we process personal data outside the data intermediary role, we must ensure that our purposes and means are consistent with any applicable notification, consent or exception under the PDPA. We will also maintain appropriate policies and procedures, assess the accuracy and relevance of personal data where required, and cooperate with data subjects and the relevant authorities.

12. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, an individual may have the right to:

  • be informed about the personal data ALATA holds about them and how it is used;
  • request access to a copy of their personal data;
  • request correction of personal data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • object to or request the restriction of certain processing;
  • request deletion or anonymisation in circumstances where the law allows; and
  • complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission of Singapore or another competent authority.

To make a request, email hello@alata.example with the subject line “Personal data request”. Please describe your request clearly and provide enough information for us to identify you and locate the relevant data. We may ask for additional verification where reasonably necessary to protect your information.

We will respond within the period required by applicable law or within a reasonable period where no specific statutory period applies. If a request is complex, we may explain why an extension or clarification is needed. There is generally no charge for a reasonable request, although a non-refundable administrative charge may apply where permitted by law, particularly for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests.

Where an enquiry concerns personal data held in our capacity as a data intermediary, we may need to direct the requester to the relevant client or assist the client in responding. We will explain this where appropriate.

13. Data from children

Our services and website are intended for businesses, business representatives and prospective employees, and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without the relevant authorisation required by law. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us inappropriately, please contact hello@alata.example.

We encourage you to review the privacy notice of each external website before sharing personal data.
