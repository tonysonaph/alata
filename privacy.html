Privacy Policy

How ALATA collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal data in accordance with the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). This document is a template pending formal legal review.

Notice: This Privacy Policy is a template prepared for internal review and has not been finalised by legal counsel. It references the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) and general best practices. ALATA Pte. Ltd. ("ALATA", "we", "us", "our") will replace this version with a legally reviewed policy prior to public launch. Contact hello@alata.example with questions.

1. Scope and Applicability

This policy applies to all personal data processed by ALATA Pte. Ltd. (UEN [UEN]), registered at [Office address], Singapore, in connection with our website (alata.example), dashboard (Orbit OS), mobile applications, APIs, events, and offline interactions with clients, prospects, partners, job applicants, and website visitors ("you"). It does not apply to third-party platforms (TikTok Shop, Shopee, Lazada) whose own privacy policies govern data they collect directly from end consumers.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data You Provide Directly

  • Account & Profile: Name, email, phone, company name, role, billing address, payment details, UEN/business registration number, platform seller IDs.
  • Communications: Messages sent via contact forms, chat, email, support tickets, sales calls, demo requests, and event registrations.
  • Onboarding & KYC: Business licences, tax IDs, authorised signatory documents, bank account verification artifacts required for payout processing.
  • Preferences: Marketing opt-ins, language, timezone, notification settings.

2.2 Data Collected Automatically

  • Usage & Telemetry: IP address, device type, browser, OS, referrer, pages visited, timestamps, feature interactions, API call volumes, error logs.
  • Cookies & Similar Tech: Essential cookies (session, auth, security, load balancing), analytics cookies (aggregated usage), preference cookies (UI state). We do not use advertising cookies on our marketing site. See Section 10 for details.
  • Dashboard Activity: Actions within Orbit OS — catalog syncs, ad campaign creation, fulfilment orders, live stream scheduling — linked to your organisation workspace.

2.3 Data from Third Parties

  • Platform APIs: Order, product, inventory, logistics, and performance data pulled from TikTok Shop, Shopee, Lazada via authorised OAuth connections you configure.
  • Service Providers: Verification results from KYC/AML vendors, payment gateway tokens, fraud signals, email deliverability metrics.
  • Public Sources: Company registry data, public marketplace listings, publicly posted case studies (with consent).

4. Disclosure and Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only as follows:

  • Processors (Data Intermediaries): Cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP), analytics (PostHog, Mixpanel), communications (SendGrid, Intercom), payments (Stripe, local PGs), KYC (Onfido, Jumio), fraud (Sift), support (Linear, Slack Connect). All processors execute DPAs with PDPA-compliant terms.
  • Platform Partners: Data you instruct us to push/pull via TikTok Shop, Shopee, Lazada APIs — governed by your authorisation and each platform's developer terms.
  • Corporate Transactions: In a merger, acquisition, financing, or asset sale, data transfers to advisors and successors under confidentiality.
  • Legal & Safety: To comply with law, regulation, court order, or government request; to protect rights, safety, or property of ALATA, users, or the public.
  • Consent-Based: Any other sharing only with your explicit consent (e.g., case study publication, partner co-marketing).

5. Cross-Border Transfers

ALATA is headquartered in Singapore. Our processors and sub-processors may operate in the United States, European Union, Australia, Japan, and other Southeast Asian countries. Where data leaves Singapore, we ensure a comparable standard of protection through:

  • EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or UK International Data Transfer Agreement;
  • APEC Cross-Border Privacy Rules (CBPR) certification where applicable;
  • Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs) for intra-group transfers (future);
  • Your explicit consent for specific transfers not covered above.

A current sub-processor list with locations and safeguards is available on request.

7. Your Rights (PDPA Access & Correction, Withdrawal, Portability)

  • Access & Correction (s.21–22): Request a copy of your personal data and correct inaccuracies. We respond within 30 days (extendable by 30 days with notice).
  • Withdrawal of Consent (s.16): Withdraw consent for marketing or optional processing at any time via dashboard settings, unsubscribe link, or email to hello@alata.example. Withdrawal does not affect processing based on other lawful bases.
  • Data Portability (s.26–27, forthcoming): Where technically feasible, receive your provided data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (JSON/CSV) or direct transfer to another controller.
  • Deletion / Restriction: Request deletion where data is no longer necessary, consent withdrawn, or processing unlawful — subject to legal retention obligations (Section 6).
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interest (e.g., analytics, security). We will assess and cease unless compelling grounds override.
  • Complaint: Lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) Singapore: pdpc.gov.sg.

To exercise any right, email hello@alata.example with "Privacy Rights Request" in the subject. We may verify identity before acting.

8. Security Measures

  • Encryption: TLS 1.2+ in transit; AES-256 at rest for databases, backups, object storage.
  • Access Control: Role-based access (RBAC), principle of least privilege, MFA enforced for all staff and client admin accounts, SSH keys only, no shared credentials.
  • Infrastructure: VPC isolation, security groups, WAF, DDoS protection, automated patching, immutable deployments.
  • Monitoring: SIEM with 24/7 alerting, anomaly detection on auth/API, annual penetration testing (CREST), quarterly vulnerability scans.
  • Data Minimisation: Pseudonymisation in non-prod; synthetic test data; no production data in staging.
  • Incident Response: Documented IR plan, 72-hour PDPC notification where required, client notification per contractual SLA.
  • Certifications (Target): SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001 — illustrative targets, not yet achieved.

9. Children's Data

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18 (or 21 where applicable). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If a client's end-consumer data includes minors (e.g., platform order data), the client remains the data controller; we process only as processor per our DPA.

12. Client Data (Processor Role)

When you connect TikTok Shop, Shopee, or Lazada stores, you are the Data Controller for end-consumer personal data (buyer names, addresses, phone numbers, order history). ALATA acts as Data Intermediary (Processor) under a Data Processing Addendum (DPA) incorporated into our Terms of Service. Key commitments:

  • Process only on your documented instructions (API scopes you enable);
  • No independent use, profiling, or marketing to your buyers;
  • Sub-processors disclosed and authorised per DPA;
  • Security measures per Section 8;
  • Breach notification within 24 hours of discovery;
  • Deletion/return of data on contract termination per DPA schedule;
  • Audit cooperation (annual self-assessment, on-site by agreement).
