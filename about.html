    01 / COMPANY

    Winged commerce for Southeast Asia.

    ALATA builds the infrastructure that lets brands and merchants sell seamlessly across TikTok Shop, Shopee, and Lazada — from first listing to full-scale operations.

    02 / ORIGIN

    Why Alata.

    Alata is Latin for "winged". The name reflects our conviction that commerce in Southeast Asia should be borderless, fast, and weightless — lifted above platform complexity and operational drag.

    The problem we saw

    Sellers expanding across TikTok Shop, Shopee, and Lazada face fragmented tooling: separate dashboards for listings, different logistics networks per platform, disjointed ad managers, and reconciliation nightmares at month-end. Most "solutions" are single-platform point tools or generic ERPs that don't speak marketplace language.

    Our approach

    We unify the stack. One control plane — Alata OS — connects every platform API, every warehouse, every creator network, and every financial rail. Sellers operate from a single workspace while we handle the translation layer beneath. The result: faster launches, lower ops cost, and measurable growth across every channel.

    03 / MISSION & VALUES

    How we operate.

    01

    Platform fluency over platform preference

    We go where the seller's customers are. Our roadmap follows demand — TikTok Shop, Shopee, Lazada, and whatever comes next in SEA.

    02

    Outcomes over activity

    Every feature ships with a measurable KPI: GMV lift, take-rate reduction, SLA improvement. If it doesn't move the needle, it doesn't ship.

    03

    Local depth, regional scale

    Singapore HQ with embedded teams in Kuala Lumpur, Manila, Bangkok, Ho Chi Minh City, Jakarta. We know the regulations, the holidays, the courier quirks.

    04

    Transparency by default

    Unified dashboards show real fees, real settlement timelines, real inventory positions. No black boxes. Sellers own their data.

    05

    Builder mindset

    We write our own integrations, run our own warehouses, train our own CS agents. Vertical integration lets us fix root causes, not file tickets.

    06

    Long-term alignment

    Our commercial model ties to seller success — not seat counts or retainer hours. We win when merchants grow profitably.
