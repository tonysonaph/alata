01 / The opportunity

New markets ask for more than a new storefront.

A successful entry depends on how the whole experience holds together: how products are presented, how campaigns are run, how orders reach customers and how every transaction is reconciled.

ALATA helps international brands build that operating foundation without losing the character that made them worth choosing in the first place. We combine marketplace expertise with local operational care, giving teams a single, accountable partner from preparation through growth.

05 / How it works

A measured path from readiness to momentum.

01

Listen & assess

We clarify the proposition, audience, category economics, operational readiness and the outcomes that matter.

  • Market and category review
  • Capability and readiness assessment
  • Prioritised launch plan
02

Prepare

We establish the storefront, product information, content, fulfilment flow and measurement foundations before launch.

  • Store and catalogue setup
  • Creative and operational preparation
  • Tracking and workflow agreements
03

Launch & learn

We enter the market with a focused offer, observe customer response and turn practical signals into better decisions.

  • Controlled marketplace launch
  • Campaign and live-commerce activity
  • Weekly learning loops
04

Scale carefully

As the model earns confidence, we extend the assortment, campaign mix and operating rhythm with governance intact.

  • Portfolio and market expansion
  • Performance-led optimisation
  • Consistent governance
