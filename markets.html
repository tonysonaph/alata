01 / MARKETS

Southeast Asia, country by country

Six markets. Three platforms each. Distinct regulations, languages, payment rails and logistics networks. ALATA translates complexity into a single operating model.

02 / SINGAPORE

Singapore — HQ & Regional Launchpad

Singapore is ALATA's home base and the most mature e-commerce environment in the region. High purchasing power, robust infrastructure and English-first commerce make it the ideal testbed for new brands entering SEA.

01

Platform Dynamics

Shopee and Lazada dominate traditional marketplace GMV; TikTok Shop grows rapidly via live commerce and affiliate-driven discovery. Singapore users expect same-day or next-day delivery as baseline. Cross-border enabled by default on all three platforms. High adoption of Shopify-to-marketplace connectors for DTC brands.

02

Payments & COD

Cash-on-delivery negligible (<2%). Cards (Visa/Mastercard/Amex), PayNow, GrabPay, ShopeePay, Lazada Wallet, Atome, GrabPayLater dominate. BNPL penetration ~18% of GMV. Platform settlement cycles: T+2 to T+5 depending on seller tier. GST (9%) applies to all B2C transactions; overseas vendor registration required for foreign sellers exceeding SGD 1M annual turnover.

03

Logistics

Dense 728 km² enables island-wide same-day via NinjaVan, J&T, SingPost, uParcel. FBS (Fulfilment by Shopee) and LGS (Lazada Global Shipping) warehouses in Jurong/Tuas. Returns rate 8–12% for apparel. Cross-border inbound via Changi air hub; 48h clearance typical. ALATA manages multi-carrier allocation and SLA monitoring from single dashboard.

04

Language & Compliance

English (official), Mandarin, Malay, Tamil. Product listings in English mandatory; bilingual EN/ZH recommended for conversion. PDPA compliance for customer data. Mandatory price display inclusive of GST. Consumer Protection (Fair Trading) Act applies. ALATA provides compliant copy templates and automated GST invoice generation.

05

ALATA Focus

  • HQ team handles entity incorporation, UEN registration, GST registration
  • Live commerce studio partnerships for TikTok Shop shows
  • BNPL integration (Atome, GrabPayLater) at checkout
  • Same-day fulfilment orchestration across 4+ carriers
  • Cross-border gateway to MY, PH, TH, VN, ID from single SG stock pool
03 / MALAYSIA

Malaysia — Bilingual Marketplace at Scale

Malaysia combines high digital adoption with bilingual (EN/MS) commerce, strong social buying behaviour and mature COD infrastructure. A natural second market for Singapore-based sellers.

01

Platform Dynamics

Shopee leads GMV (~55%), Lazada strong (~30%), TikTok Shop accelerating (~15%). Ramadan/Hari Raya, 11.11, 12.12 are peak periods. Shopee Mall and LazMall authenticity badges drive conversion. Live commerce thrives on TikTok Shop with Malay-language hosts. Cross-border from SG/TH/CN significant.

02

Payments & COD

COD remains ~25% of orders (higher in East Malaysia). FPX (bank transfer), Touch 'n Go, Boost, ShopeePay, Lazada Wallet, GrabPay, Atome, SPayLater. BNPL ~22% of paid GMV. Platform settlement T+3 to T+7. SST (6% services, 10% goods) applies; foreign sellers register via MySST if annual taxable turnover > RM500k.

03

Logistics

Peninsular Malaysia: next-day standard via NinjaVan, J&T, CityLink, DHL, PosLaju. East Malaysia (Sabah/Sarawak): 3–5 days, surcharge applies. FBS and LGS warehouses in Shah Alam, Ipoh, Johor. Return rates 10–15% (apparel). Cross-border inbound via KLIA/KUL and land checkpoints. ALATA optimises split inventory Peninsular vs East Malaysia.

04

Language & Compliance

Bahasa Melayu (official), English widely used. Listings in BM mandatory for Shopee Mall/LazMall; EN accepted elsewhere. Consumer Protection Act 1999, PDPA 2010. Halal certification required for food/cosmetics (JAKIM). Price display inclusive of SST. ALATA provides BM/EN copy, Halal cert liaison, SST invoice automation.

05

ALATA Focus

  • Dual-language content production (BM/EN) at scale
  • COD reconciliation and fraud screening workflows
  • East Malaysia surcharge optimisation and inventory split
  • Ramadan/Hari Raya campaign calendar and affiliate activation
  • Halal certification coordination for eligible categories
04 / PHILIPPINES

Philippines — Social Commerce Heartland

The Philippines drives the region's highest social commerce engagement. English fluency, mobile-first population and creator-led buying make TikTok Shop a primary channel alongside Shopee/Lazada.

01

Platform Dynamics

Shopee ~60% GMV, Lazada ~20%, TikTok Shop ~20% and growing fastest. 7.7, 8.8, 9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12 monthly sales culture. Live commerce dominant: 4–6 hour daily shows typical for top sellers. Affiliate/creator commissions 10–30% standard. Shopee Mall/LazMall trust badges critical. Cross-border from CN/SG/TH via LGS/SLS.

02

Payments & COD

COD ~40% of orders nationwide (higher outside NCR). GCash, Maya, ShopeePay, Lazada Wallet, GrabPay, Billease, Atome. BNPL ~15% of paid GMV. Platform settlement T+5 to T+10. VAT 12% inclusive; foreign sellers register via BIR if annual sales > PHP 3M. Withholding tax (1–2%) on platform payouts.

03

Logistics

Archipelago of 7,600+ islands. NCR/Metro Manila: same-day/next-day via NinjaVan, J&T, Flash, XDE, LBC. Provincial: 3–7 days. Shopee Xpress (SPX) and Lazada LGS cover last mile. Returns 12–18% (apparel). COD remittance cycles 7–14 days. ALATA manages island-group inventory allocation (Luzon/Visayas/Mindanao) and COD cashflow forecasting.

04

Language & Compliance

Filipino (Tagalog) and English official. Listings in EN standard; Tagalog/Taglish boosts conversion in live/social. DTI Business Name Registration for local entity. FDA registration for food, cosmetics, supplements. Consumer Act (RA 7394). Data Privacy Act 2012. ALATA produces EN/Taglish scripts for live hosts, handles FDA LTO coordination.

05

ALATA Focus

  • Creator network: 200+ vetted affiliates across beauty, fashion, home, electronics
  • Live show production: scheduling, host briefing, real-time GMV tracking
  • COD cashflow management: remittance forecasting, dispute reduction
  • Island-group inventory strategy: 3PL partners in Luzon, Visayas, Mindanao
  • FDA License to Operate (LTO) and CPR registration for regulated categories
05 / THAILAND

Thailand — Live Commerce Leader

Thailand pioneered live commerce in SEA. TikTok Shop GMV share leads the region. Thai language, unique payment rails (PromptPay) and dense Bangkok logistics create a distinct operating model.

01

Platform Dynamics

TikTok Shop ~35% GMV (highest in SEA), Shopee ~45%, Lazada ~20%. Live commerce mature: professional agencies, dedicated studios, 8–12 hour daily broadcasts. 9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12, Songkran (Apr) key moments. Shopee Mall/LazMall trust signals. Cross-border from CN dominant; SG/MY growing via CBEC.

02

Payments & COD

COD ~15% (declining). PromptPay (QR/bank) ~50% of non-COD. TrueMoney, Rabbit LINE Pay, ShopeePay, Lazada Wallet, Atome, Krungsri First Choice. BNPL ~18%. Platform settlement T+2 to T+5. VAT 7% (rising to 10% TBD); foreign sellers register via RD if annual revenue > THB 1.8M. Withholding tax 3% on platform fees.

03

Logistics

Bangkok metro: same-day via Kerry, Flash, NinjaVan, J&T, SCG. Provincial: next-day to 3 days. FBS (Shopee) and LGS (Lazada) warehouses in Bang Phli, Wang Noi. TikTok Shop uses 3PL network (Kerry, Flash, NinjaVan). Returns 10–14%. Cross-border inbound via Suvarnabhumi/DMK; 24–48h clearance. ALATA coordinates multi-platform fulfilment from unified Bangkok hub.

04

Language & Compliance

Thai (official). Listings in Thai mandatory for conversion; EN supplementary. PDPA (2022) full enforcement. Consumer Protection Act. FDA Thailand for food/cosmetics/medical devices. E-commerce Act B.E. 2544. Price display VAT-inclusive. ALATA provides Thai-native copy, live scripts, PDPA-compliant data flows, FDA registration.

05

ALATA Focus

  • Live commerce studio partnerships in Bangkok (3 dedicated partners)
  • Thai-native host roster for TikTok Shop and Shopee Live
  • PromptPay QR generation and reconciliation automation
  • FDA Thailand notification/registration for regulated SKUs
  • Cross-border CBEC setup from CN/SG/MY into Thailand
06 / VIETNAM

Vietnam — Fastest-Growing Marketplace Economy

Vietnam's young demographic, rising middle class and platform-first shopping habits drive 25%+ YoY e-commerce growth. Zalo ecosystem, COD dominance and northern/southern logistics split require localised execution.

01

Platform Dynamics

Shopee ~65% GMV, TikTok Shop ~25% (rapid growth), Lazada ~10%. 3.3, 6.6, 7.7, 8.8, 9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12 monthly mega-sales. Live commerce on TikTok Shop and Shopee Live growing 3x YoY. KOL/KOC ecosystem on Zalo, Facebook, TikTok. Shopee Mall authenticity critical. Cross-border from CN via SLS; SG/MY via CBEC.

02

Payments & COD

COD ~50% of orders (highest in SEA). MoMo, ZaloPay, ShopeePay, VNPay, ViettelPay, HomeCredit, Atome (VN). BNPL ~12%. Platform settlement T+5 to T+10. VAT 10% (8% temporary reduction 2024–2025); foreign sellers register via GDT if revenue > VND 1B. Withholding tax 5% on cross-border platform fees.

03

Logistics

North (Hanoi) vs South (HCMC) inventory split essential. GHN (GHN), GHTK, J&T, NinjaVan, Viettel Post cover last mile. Shopee Xpress (SPX) dominant for Shopee orders. FBS warehouses in Bac Ninh (North), Binh Duong (South). Returns 15–20% (apparel). COD remittance 7–14 days. ALATA manages dual-hub inventory and COD cashflow per region.

04

Language & Compliance

Vietnamese (official). Listings in Vietnamese mandatory. Decree 52/2013/ND-CP (e-commerce), Decree 85/2021/ND-CP (cross-border), Cybersecurity Law, PDPD (Decree 13/2023). FDA Vietnam for food/cosmetics. Price display VAT-inclusive. Business registration (ERC) required for local entity. ALATA provides VN copy, Decree 85 CBEC compliance, FDA registration.

05

ALATA Focus

  • North/South dual-hub inventory optimisation (Bac Ninh / Binh Duong)
  • COD reconciliation at scale: automated dispute filing, remittance tracking
  • Zalo Official Account setup and CRM integration for retention
  • Decree 85 cross-border e-commerce compliance for CN/SG/MY inbound
  • Live commerce localisation: VN hosts, scripts, scheduling for TikTok/Shopee Live
07 / INDONESIA

Indonesia — Archipelago Scale, Platform Diversity

Indonesia is SEA's largest e-commerce market by GMV and population. 17,000+ islands, diverse languages, dominant Shopee/Tokopedia (merged), rising TikTok Shop, and complex logistics demand a fragmented but coordinated approach.

01

Platform Dynamics

Shopee (incl. Tokopedia) ~70% GMV combined, TikTok Shop ~20%, Lazada ~10%. 3.3, 4.4, 5.5, 6.6, 7.7, 8.8, 9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12. Tokopedia strong in electronics/home; Shopee in fashion/beauty. TikTok Shop live commerce explosive (100%+ YoY). Affiliate/creator commissions 15–35%. Cross-border via Shopee International Platform (SIP) and TikTok Shop CBEC.

02

Payments & COD

COD ~30% (higher outside Java). GoPay, ShopeePay, Dana, OVO, LinkAja, Kredivo, Akulaku, Atome, BCA KlikPay. BNPL ~25% of paid GMV (Kredivo, Akulaku lead). Platform settlement T+3 to T+7. VAT 11% (12% from 2025); foreign sellers register via DJP if annual transaction > IDR 600M. Income tax Article 23 (2%) on platform fees.

03

Logistics

Java (Jabodetabek, Bandung, Surabaya): same-day/next-day via J&T, JNE, SiCepat, NinjaVan, Lion Parcel. Outer islands: 3–10 days. Shopee/ Tokopedia fulfilment centres in West Java, East Java. TikTok Shop uses 3PL network. Returns 12–18%. COD remittance 7–14 days. ALATA manages Java vs outer-island inventory tiers and carrier allocation by SLA/cost.

04

Language & Compliance

Bahasa Indonesia (official). Listings in Bahasa mandatory. PDP Law (UU 27/2022) enforcement 2024. Consumer Protection Law (UU 8/1999). Halal mandatory (Law 33/2014) for food/cosmetics/fashion — BPJPH certification. BPOM registration for food/cosmetics/health. Price display VAT-inclusive. ALATA provides Bahasa copy, Halal/BPJPH coordination, BPOM registration, PDP-compliant data handling.

05

ALATA Focus

  • Java vs outer-island inventory tiering (3PL partners in 12 cities)
  • Halal certification (BPJPH) and BPOM registration end-to-end
  • Tokopedia + Shopee unified catalogue management post-merger
  • BNPL integration: Kredivo, Akulaku, Atome at checkout
  • TikTok Shop live commerce: local agency partnerships, Bahasa hosts
08 / CROSS-BORDER

Cross-Border: One Stock Pool, Six Markets

ALATA's cross-border model lets you hold inventory in one or two strategic hubs (Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand) and sell across all six markets via platform CBEC programmes — no local entity required for market entry.

01

CBEC Models by Platform

  • Shopee: SIP (Shopee International Platform) — ship from CN/HK/SG/MY/TH warehouses to 6 markets. SLS (Shopee Logistics Service) handles last mile.
  • Lazada: LGS (Lazada Global Shipping) — ship from CN/HK/SG/MY/TH to PH, TH, VN, MY, SG, ID. LGS sorts in Hong Kong/Shenzhen hub.
  • TikTok Shop: CBEC (Cross-Border E-Commerce) — ship from CN/HK/SG/MY/TH bonded warehouses. Platform-appointed 3PL for last mile.
02

Hub Strategy

  • Singapore Hub: Best for SG, MY, PH, ID (air hub, 24–48h clearance). High labour cost, premium speed.
  • Malaysia Hub (Port Klang/Shah Alam): Best for MY, SG, ID (land + sea), TH (land). Lower cost, 2–4 day regional delivery.
  • Thailand Hub (Bangkok): Best for TH, MY, VN (land), ID (sea). Strong for TikTok Shop CBEC.
  • ALATA runs network optimisation: demand forecasting per market → recommended hub split → automated replenishment.
03

Compliance & Tax

  • De minimis thresholds: SG (SGD 400), MY (RM500), PH (PHP 10k), TH (THB 1,500), VN (VND 1M), ID (USD 3 / 75k IDR for CBEC).
  • VAT/GST/SST collection: Platforms collect at checkout for CBEC orders in SG, MY, PH, TH, VN, ID. Seller remits via platform.
  • HS code classification critical for duty/tax accuracy. ALATA maintains 6-market HS code library.
  • Product compliance per market: Halal (MY/ID), FDA (PH/TH/VN/ID), BPOM (ID), CPR (PH), PDPA (TH/VN/ID/SG/MY).
