Solutions / End-to-end commerce

Every part of the journey, working together.

ALATA brings strategy, creative, marketplace operations, fulfilment and finance into one coordinated service—giving brands a more considered way to enter and grow across Southeast Asia.

01 / A connected model

Less hand-off. More momentum.

Selling across marketplaces is not simply a matter of opening more storefronts. Products, inventory, content, campaigns, customer conversations and cash flow must all reflect the same commercial plan.

ALATA assigns an operating rhythm around your business. We begin with the channels, products and markets that matter most, then build the work needed to support them. Teams work from shared briefs, visible priorities and clear hand-offs—so decisions do not disappear between disciplines.

  • One accountable lead across commercial and operational workstreams
  • Marketplace-native execution with a consistent brand voice
  • Processes designed around the real stages of marketplace selling
  • Regular reviews focused on decisions, opportunities and next actions

02 / Store setup & onboarding

A strong foundation, from the first listing.

We prepare the storefront, operating structure and launch plan so your team begins with a clear path to market.

01

What we do

Our team reviews your commercial model, product catalogue, brand guidelines, service capacity and target markets. From there, we configure the practical foundations required for selling through TikTok Shop, Shopee and Lazada.

Channel setup is approached as an operating design exercise—not a checklist. We establish ownership, information flows, service expectations and launch controls that can be maintained after onboarding.

02

Deliverables

  • Marketplace onboarding, seller registration support and account configuration
  • Store profile, navigation, policies, service settings and brand information
  • Category, attribute and listing requirement mapping
  • SKU, inventory, pricing, tax and fulfilment information templates
  • Launch calendar covering content, stock, campaign and customer readiness
  • Channel operating procedures, escalation paths and reporting cadence
  • Issue log and onboarding handover pack
Outcome

A marketplace presence designed to be managed, not merely opened.

Your team gains a considered storefront setup, a documented operating model and a launch plan that connects commercial ambition with the realities of inventory, service and fulfilment.

04 / Live commerce & creator partnerships

Live moments, built around real connection.

We help brands create live shopping and creator programmes that feel useful, credible and aligned with the product—not like a repeated sales pitch.

How we work

From session idea to managed delivery

Our live commerce team develops the commercial plan, content flow, product mix, offer structure and operating checklist. We coordinate hosts, creators and internal teams around the moments that need to move quickly.

For affiliate and KOL activity, we can support creator identification, outreach, briefing, content review, campaign tracking and performance reporting. Participation is subject to creator availability, audience fit, commercial terms and platform requirements.

Deliverables
  • Live commerce strategy, objectives and session calendar
  • Run-of-show, talking points and product demonstration flow
  • Host, moderator and key operator coordination
  • Product sequence, offer recommendations and promotion setup
  • Creator sourcing, briefing and collaboration support
  • Campaign tracking links and post-campaign review
  • Live and creator content repurposing recommendations
Outcome

A more human buying experience—with the detail to support it.

Customers can understand products through live demonstrations and creator perspectives, while your team retains control of the offer, brief, run of show and post-session learning.

05 / Advertising & marketplace campaigns

Campaigns with a commercial reason.

We connect paid media, marketplace campaigns, promotional mechanics and inventory planning so each push is supported by the operational work behind it.

01

Always-on advertising

Campaign structures are built around clear commercial roles: creating visibility, encouraging product discovery, supporting live activity, remarketing engaged shoppers and protecting efficient promotion periods.

We monitor platform changes, pacing and delivery patterns, then recommend adjustments with the catalogue, stock position and offer calendar in view.

02

Mega sale-day readiness

For major marketplace moments such as 9.9, 11.11 and 12.12, we help translate the campaign calendar into specific pricing, content, stock, service and fulfilment plans.

Each campaign is reviewed for operational readiness before launch, with a clear escalation route for issues that may arise during the live period.

Deliverables
  • Advertising structure, naming conventions and channel ownership
  • Campaign briefs with offer, audience, content and stock requirements
  • 9.9, 11.11 and 12.12 campaign preparation plans
  • Promotion and voucher setup support
  • Budget allocation guidance and pacing review
  • Live campaign monitoring and issue escalation
  • Post-campaign performance review and next-step recommendations
Outcome

Advertising activity that is easier to manage and more accountable.

Your team sees where spend is directed, what operational support each campaign needs and how results are assessed. Decisions are informed by commercial performance without losing sight of margin, stock and customer experience.

Advertising results depend on market conditions, inventory, pricing, creative quality, platform delivery and other factors outside ALATA’s control. No outcome is guaranteed.

06 / Fulfilment & cross-border logistics

From confirmed order to peace of mind.

We coordinate the physical movement of products with the same care we bring to the customer-facing journey.

01

Inventory readiness

Forecast inputs, channel allocation, available-to-sell quantities and launch buffers are aligned before promotions begin.

02

Order flow

Orders are released according to agreed service levels, with warehouse, channel and customer information kept aligned.

03

Pack and dispatch

Packing requirements, product protection, documentation and marketplace handover are managed against the agreed operating plan.

04

Track and resolve

Delivery exceptions are monitored and routed to the right partner or internal owner, with customer updates connected to the order status.

Cross-border

Movement designed around the market

For cross-border fulfilment, we support the practical decisions that shape cost, speed and customer experience: inventory location, routing, customs documentation, restricted-item checks, carrier handoffs and local delivery expectations.

Routes and service levels are subject to product eligibility, destination requirements, carrier capability and operational availability.

Deliverables
  • Inventory and allocation planning support
  • Order processing and warehouse coordination
  • Packaging, labelling and dispatch requirements
  • Carrier and 3PL coordination
  • Cross-border route and customs-document readiness
  • Delivery tracking and exception handling
  • Returns and reverse-logistics coordination
  • Operational performance review
Outcome

A more dependable order journey, with fewer surprises between systems.

Inventory, marketplace, warehouse, carrier and customer-service activity are connected through clear processes and shared status updates—giving your team better visibility when fulfilment needs attention.

09 / Finance & COD reconciliation

Clarity from order to settlement.

We help reconcile marketplace transactions, cash on delivery, fees, refunds, adjustments and payouts so commercial reporting tells a more complete story.

01

Transaction reconciliation

Order-level records are matched against marketplace statements and operational events. Unusual values are investigated and routed for resolution rather than left buried in a spreadsheet.

02

COD and payout tracking

For eligible cash-on-delivery models, we support the operational view of collections, remittance, delivery outcomes and unsettled balances across relevant parties.

Deliverables
  • Order, transaction and payout data collection
  • Marketplace fee and deduction mapping
  • COD collection and remittance tracking support
  • Refund, cancellation and chargeback review
  • Reconciliation exceptions log with ownership
  • Settlement and variance reporting
  • Finance handover pack and recurring close support
Outcome

A clearer view of what was sold, fulfilled and settled.

Your team can identify unmatched records and understand the path from order to payout. Reconciliation becomes an ongoing control rather than an end-of-period scramble.

Reconciliation support reflects the systems, documentation and transaction access made available. It does not constitute accounting, tax, legal or financial advice.

Best for brands preparing a considered entry into one or more Southeast Asian marketplaces.

How we define the scope

Clear responsibilities from the outset.

Every engagement begins with agreed outcomes, workstreams, channel coverage, service levels, deliverables, reporting and decision rights. Commercial fees, third-party costs and any platform charges are set out transparently in the written scope.

Where an engagement is sold as a percentage of sales, the associated revenue share, ad spend, creator fees, discounts, refunds, taxes, duties and other applicable costs should be reviewed carefully so the commercial model remains clear to all parties.
