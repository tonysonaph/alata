Technology / The operating view

Clarity for every market.

ALATA brings storefronts, stock, orders, campaigns, customer conversations, and finance into one considered operating view—so your team can make better decisions without learning another maze of tools.

01 / A shared centre of gravity

Less switching.
More seeing.

Every marketplace creates its own language, workflow, and rhythm. Our role is not to add more noise. It is to give teams a consistent view of what is happening across their channel portfolio.

The ALATA dashboard is designed around the questions commercial teams ask every day: Which products are moving? Where should inventory sit? What needs attention before a customer does? How is each campaign landing? The answer should be available without assembling a spreadsheet, opening six tabs, and asking three colleagues.

    02 / Inventory synchronisation

    Stock that stays honest.

    Inventory should move at the speed of your business, not the speed of a spreadsheet refresh. ALATA helps keep product availability aligned across connected storefronts and fulfilment locations.

    When a sale is made, stock is reconciled against the relevant pool. When a catalogue changes, listings can be updated with the right product information, variants, and media. When supply is constrained, the system helps surface the effect before unavailable products become avoidable customer friction.

    01

    Channel-aware updates

    Keep supported storefronts informed of available stock and listing status, with platform-specific constraints taken into account.

    02

    Pool visibility

    See inventory by product, variant, location, and channel to make clearer allocation decisions.

    03

    Exception-led care

    Identify overselling risk, low-stock products, stalled variants, and mismatches that need a human check.

    04

    Human oversight

    Automation handles the routine while your team approves important adjustments and resolves edge cases.

    03 / Order routing

    The right fulfilment path, chosen early.

    Orders can begin as a simple checkout, then become a chain of handoffs: warehouse pick, pack, cross-border movement, local delivery, and customer update. ALATA helps organise that journey around clear rules.

    Routing can reflect the order destination, product characteristics, available inventory, service promise, and operational capability. Rules can be reviewed by people, exceptions can be escalated, and fulfilment partners can receive a consistent brief. The aim is not complexity for its own sake; it is a process your team can understand.

    01

    Capture

    Receive order details in a normalised format across connected channels.

    02

    Decide

    Apply agreed routing rules for stock, destination, product, and service level.

    03

    Dispatch

    Send a clear fulfilment brief and track the next operational milestone.

    04

    Reconcile

    Bring fulfilment charges and order outcomes back into the commercial view.

    06 / Security and data principles

    Trust is part of the infrastructure.

    Commerce data can be commercially sensitive, operationally important, and personal. We treat its stewardship as a core product requirement—not an add-on.

    ALATA’s operating approach begins with collecting only what is needed for agreed commercial and service purposes. Access should be limited, attributable, and reviewed. Sensitive information should be protected in transit and at rest. Operational teams should be able to see where data comes from, what has changed, and when an issue requires escalation.

    01

    Least privilege

    Grant access according to role and responsibility, with regular review and prompt removal when it is no longer needed.

    02

    Encryption

    Use appropriate technical controls to protect data during transfer and storage, including sensitive credentials.

    03

    Auditability

    Maintain appropriate logs of administrative and operational actions to support review and investigation.

    04

    Data minimisation

    Limit collection and retention to information with a clear business purpose and an agreed lifecycle.

    05

    Responsible AI

    Restrict automated outputs to approved use cases and keep consequential decisions subject to human review.

    06

    Resilient operations

    Prepare backup, recovery, vendor, and incident processes proportionate to the importance of the service.

    Specific certifications, contractual commitments, hosting locations, retention periods, and incident response timelines should be confirmed during due diligence. This page describes product principles and is not a technical assurance report.

    Technology questions

    A few useful answers.

    Does the dashboard replace our marketplace tools?

    No. The dashboard is intended to provide a consistent commercial and operational view across channels. Each marketplace may still offer its own native tools for functions that require platform-level access. ALATA can complement those tools with normalised information, workflow support, and a coordinated view of work in progress.

    How is inventory synchronisation handled?

    Inventory is reconciled according to the products, variants, locations, and channels included in each client’s agreed scope. The operating process should include checks for exceptions such as overselling risk, delayed stock feeds, manual adjustments, and platform-specific listing constraints.

    Can the AI tools publish content or change prices automatically?

    AI tools are designed primarily to assist. Generated listing copy, pricing guidance, forecasts, and customer-service drafts can be reviewed by the appropriate team before publication or implementation. Approval policies can be defined around your operating requirements.

    What information is required to begin an integration?

    Discovery usually covers channel access, catalogue structure, order volumes, fulfilment locations, finance processes, service workflows, and reporting needs. We then map data fields, ownership, update frequency, exception handling, and any third-party dependencies before implementation begins.

    Where is customer and commerce data hosted?

    Hosting and data residency arrangements depend on the services, vendors, and markets included in a client’s solution. These details should be documented during due diligence rather than assumed. Contact us to discuss the appropriate requirements for your business.

    Can ALATA support one market before expanding?

    Yes. A phased approach can be useful for brands that want to establish a repeatable operating rhythm before adding more channels or markets. The scope, sequence, and resource requirements should be agreed after reviewing your catalogue, order profile, and internal capacity.
