Structuring a repeatable live commerce calendar for TikTok Shop SEA

Most sellers treat live streams as one-off events. This playbook outlines a quarterly calendar framework: theme selection tied to platform mega campaigns (9.9, 11.11, 12.12), host rotation schedules to prevent fatigue, SKU bundling logic that protects margin while hitting GMV targets, and pre-stream promotional mechanics (countdown coupons, affiliate seeding, creator co-streams). We detail the 72-hour pre-flight checklist — inventory allocation by warehouse, OBS scene configuration, chat moderation rules, and real-time offer triggers tied to viewer milestones. Post-stream, we map the attribution window: first 24 hours for impulse buys, 7-day for considered purchases, and how to feed conversion data back into the next cycle's SKU selection. Illustrative targets: 18–22% live-attributed GMV share within two quarters for fashion and beauty categories.

Operations 08 Jan 2026

COD reconciliation: closing the gap between platform settlement and bank deposit

Cash-on-delivery remains 35–55% of orders in Indonesia, Philippines, and Vietnam, yet reconciliation breaks down at three points: courier collection variance, platform fee deductions applied before remittance, and return-to-origin (RTO) cash not recovered. We present a four-ledger matching model: order-level COD amount captured at checkout, courier POD-confirmed collection, platform settlement report line items, and bank statement deposits. The critical control is daily exception queues for amount mismatches >2% or aging >T+3. Automation rules: auto-match exact amounts, flag partial collections for finance review, trigger RTO write-off workflows after 14 days. We also cover VAT invoice generation for COD sales in Thailand and Vietnam where platform-issued documents don't meet local tax authority requirements. Illustrative targets: 99.2% auto-match rate, <0.5% unreconciled write-off.

Growth 20 Dec 2025

Mega campaign readiness: the 6-week countdown to 11.11 and 12.12

Platform mega campaigns compress 30% of annual GMV into 72 hours. This article breaks the preparation into six weekly workstreams. Week 6: demand forecasting by SKU using prior-year sell-through, platform traffic estimates, and marketing spend commitments — output is a purchase order plan locked with suppliers. Week 5: warehouse slotting for velocity SKUs, pre-pack kits for top 50 bundles, SLA agreements with 3PLs for 24-hour despatch. Week 4: creative production — platform-native assets (vertical video for TikTok Shop Feed, carousel for Shopee Mall, banner specs for Lazada), localized across TH, ID, VN, PH, MY, SG. Week 3: affiliate and creator contracts signed, tracking links generated, commission tiers approved. Week 2: ad account structure rebuilt for campaign objectives (ROAS vs volume), budget caps set, automated rules configured. Week 1: end-to-end dry run — checkout flow, voucher stacking logic, customer service surge scripts. Illustrative targets: 98% on-time despatch, <1.5% cancellation rate during peak.

Technology 12 Dec 2025

Listing SEO across TikTok Shop, Shopee, and Lazada: a unified keyword framework

Each platform indexes differently: TikTok Shop weights video transcripts and hashtag clusters; Shopee prioritizes attribute completeness and search term relevance in title/description; Lazada leans on category breadcrumb adherence and seller score signals. We propose a single source-of-truth keyword matrix per SPU: primary keyword (high volume, high intent), three secondary modifiers (use case, material, occasion), and platform-specific long-tail variants. The workflow: monthly keyword harvest from platform search suggest, competitor top-100 listings, and Google Trends SEA. Title construction rules per platform character limit with mandatory attribute inclusion. Description blocks: hook (first 80 chars), benefit bullets (3–5), trust signals (warranty, auth), and hidden keyword bank in alt-text/JSON-LD where supported. Image naming convention and alt-text schema included. Illustrative targets: 40% organic search impression lift within 60 days of optimization.

Growth 28 Nov 2025

Cross-border compliance checklist: selling from Singapore into TH, ID, VN, PH, MY

Singapore-based sellers face five distinct regulatory regimes when fulfilling cross-border. Thailand: FDA pre-approval for cosmetics/supplements, Thai-language labeling, E-Customs HS code registration. Indonesia: BPOM notification for ingestibles, SNI certification for electronics, local importer of record (IOR) mandatory for non-instant goods. Vietnam: Ministry of Industry and Trade registration for cosmetics, Vietnamese labeling, Decree 15/2020 e-commerce license for foreign sellers. Philippines: FDA CPR for health/beauty, DTI-BTRCP for consumer products, BOC valuation rules for duties. Malaysia: NPRA registration for cosmetics, SIRIM for electronics, Halal certification where applicable. We map the document package per category, lead times (30–120 days), and the role of a local authorized representative. Also covered: platform-level cross-border program requirements (TikTok Shop CBT, Shopee XB, Lazada CBEC) and how they shift liability. Illustrative targets: 100% pre-launch compliance, zero customs holds.

Technology 15 Nov 2025

AI operations: from rule-based automation to predictive inventory allocation

Rule-based automation (if stock < 50 then reorder) fails under demand volatility. We outline a three-stage maturity model. Stage 1: deterministic rules — reorder points, auto-listing pausing, voucher budget caps. Stage 2: statistical models — Prophet-based demand forecasts per SKU per warehouse incorporating platform ad spend, seasonality, and competitor price tracking; output feeds suggested purchase quantities and inter-warehouse transfer recommendations. Stage 3: reinforcement learning allocation — a simulator trained on historical fulfilment data learns optimal inventory distribution across SG, MY, TH, ID, VN, PH warehouses to minimize stockout cost + holding cost + cross-border shipping cost. The agent receives daily state (inventory levels, inbound ETAs, platform traffic forecasts) and outputs transfer/purchase actions. We detail the feature store, reward function, and guardrails (max transfer qty, min weeks of cover). Human-in-the-loop approval for actions >$10k. Illustrative targets: 15% reduction in lost sales, 8% inventory carry reduction.
