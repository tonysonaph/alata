Careers / Singapore and beyond

Do work that moves commerce further.

We are building an end-to-end commerce partner for brands entering Southeast Asia. That means solving practical problems, bringing people together and making ambitious ideas feel possible.

01 / Why ALATA

A career with room to make a difference.

Commerce is not only about listings, campaigns and fulfilment. It is about understanding people: the customer discovering a product, the creator building trust, the operator resolving an order and the brand deciding what comes next.

At ALATA, you will work across these connections. You may help prepare a store for launch, support a live session, improve a fulfilment process or make sense of marketplace data. The work is varied because the opportunity is varied.

We value clear thinking, generous collaboration and the discipline to follow through. We also value candour: saying what is working, naming what is not and making the next decision easier for everyone involved.

02 / Our culture

Serious about the work. Human about the way we do it.

We are a growing, cross-functional team united by care for the work and care for the people doing it.

  • Start with the real problem.Listen closely, ask better questions and understand what a solution needs to change in practice.
  • Make the work visible.Share context, progress and decisions so colleagues and clients can move with confidence.
  • Own the outcome.Take responsibility beyond your task, especially when the detail affects a customer or a partner.
  • Challenge with respect.Offer thoughtful disagreement without making the conversation personal.
  • Keep learning in public.Share useful knowledge and invite others to improve the process with you.
  • Build for the long term.Choose durable habits over quick fixes, especially where trust is involved.
    • 03 / Working here

    What you can expect from the day to day.

    A role at ALATA is likely to involve a mix of focused delivery, collaboration and customer-facing judgement.

    01

    Work close to the work

    You will understand how a decision travels from a conversation with a client to a marketplace listing, a live broadcast, a packed parcel or a monthly report.

    02

    Learn across disciplines

    Commerce connects operations, creative, technology, finance and customer experience. We encourage people to build context beyond their own function.

    03

    Take part in the decisions

    We involve the people closest to the work, invite useful input early and explain the reasoning behind choices so learning becomes part of delivery.

    04

    Use judgement, not just process

    Playbooks help us stay consistent, but marketplaces, brands and customer needs change. Good judgement is essential when the guide does not have every answer.

    05

    Communicate with care

    Clear writing, useful updates and attentive listening are part of the job. So is knowing when to ask, when to act and when to bring someone in.

    06

    See the effect of your work

    You will have opportunities to follow projects through, hear the feedback and understand what improved because a thoughtful detail was handled well.

    06 / Hiring process

    A clear path, with no surprises.

    Our process is designed to help you understand the role, meet the people you may work with and make an informed decision at every stage.

    01

    Application review

    We review your application against the role’s actual requirements. If there is a potential fit, we will contact you to discuss your experience, availability and expectations.

    02

    Introductory conversation

    You will speak with our Talent team or the hiring lead. This is a chance to understand the work, our culture and what you want from your next role.

    03

    Working conversation

    You will discuss relevant experience and complete a practical discussion or task. We aim to assess how you approach real problems, not test obscure knowledge or performance under pressure.

    04

    Meet the team

    You will meet future collaborators and learn how the role connects to the wider business. We welcome your questions and encourage you to assess the team as carefully as we assess you.

    05

    Offer and decision

    If both sides see a strong fit, we will provide a written offer covering compensation, scope, benefits and working arrangements. You will have time to consider it and ask us questions.

    Process timings vary by role and availability. We will keep you informed as we move forward and explain if a stage changes.

    07 / Our commitments

    What you can expect from our side.

    A good recruitment process should create confidence in the person doing the hiring. These are the commitments we make to every candidate.

  • Transparent pay ranges.Before your first interview, we will share the available compensation range for the role, including its stated basis, so you can decide whether the conversation is worth pursuing.
  • A response within 10 working days.After you apply, we aim to acknowledge your application and explain the next step within 10 working days. If we need longer, we will update you and give a realistic revised timeline.
  • Your information stays relevant.We will ask only for information connected to the role, explain how it will be used and handle it with appropriate care.
  • No hidden assessments.Practical tasks will be explained in advance, relevant to the role and used only for hiring purposes. You may request context if any part is unclear.
  • Feedback where possible.We aim to offer useful feedback after interviews and final-stage discussions, while balancing the privacy of other candidates and the needs of a small team.
  • An accessible process.If you need an adjustment, a different format or additional time, tell us. We will work with you to make the process fair and accessible.
    • A note about safety

    ALATA will never ask candidates for payment.

    We will never ask a candidate to pay for a job, interview, assessment, application, equipment, travel, training, background check or offer.

    All genuine communication about a role will come from an @alata.example email address or a clearly identified member of our Talent team. We will not use payment links, gift cards, cryptocurrency or money-transfer services as part of recruitment.

    If you receive a request for money, sensitive identity documents through an unofficial channel or access to a personal bank account, do not proceed. Report the communication to careers@alata.example and retain relevant messages for review.

    08 / Frequently asked

    Questions before you apply.

    Are the roles on this page currently open?

    They are sample listings, not confirmed vacancies. They show the kind of responsibilities and experience we expect for future or illustrative hiring needs. A role is only open once its listing has been updated and a specific application link is available.

    Where are ALATA roles based?

    These sample roles are based in Singapore and indicate a hybrid working arrangement where stated. Actual location and working arrangements will depend on the confirmed role, team needs and the employment arrangement discussed with you.

    Will I receive the salary range before applying?

    Yes. We aim to share the available compensation range before your first interview, together with the basis on which it is expressed. If a range changes materially during the process, we will discuss the update openly.

    What should I include in my application?

    For the sample listings, please send a concise introduction, your relevant experience, an updated CV and any work samples that help demonstrate your capability. You do not need to submit a cover letter unless the specific role says otherwise.

    How long will the hiring process take?

    Timing depends on the role and the availability of the team. We aim to acknowledge applications within 10 working days, provide a clear timeline for each stage and update you if circumstances change.

    Will there be a practical assessment?

    Some roles include a practical discussion or task designed around the actual responsibilities. We will explain the purpose, format and time expectation beforehand. Assessments will be relevant, proportionate and connected to the role.

    Can I ask for an adjustment during the process?

    Yes. Tell us what would help you take part fully. We can discuss interview format, timing, communication methods and other reasonable adjustments. Early notice is useful but is not always required.

    Does ALATA provide visa sponsorship?

    Whether sponsorship is possible depends on the specific role, the location and applicable employment requirements. Please raise this during the introductory conversation so we can provide a clear answer as early as possible.

    How do I report a fraudulent job offer?

    Email careers@alata.example with the sender details, links, job title and any messages you received. Please do not send payment, share banking credentials or purchase gift cards. We will review the report and advise on appropriate next steps.
