01 / CAREERS

Build the infrastructure of Southeast Asian commerce

We are a small, high-density team solving hard problems at the intersection of marketplace dynamics, real-time data, and cross-border logistics. Every role carries disproportionate impact.

02 / CULTURE

How we operate

Culture is not perks. It is the set of defaults that decide what gets built, who gets heard, and how fast we learn.

01

Ownership over process

We hire builders who define the problem, ship the fix, and own the outcome. No ticket factories. No handoff theatre. If you see a gap, you fill it.

02

Truth over comfort

Radical candour is a job requirement. We debate fiercely on ideas, not people. Decisions are documented with rationale so future us can audit the thinking.

03

Speed with craft

Move fast, but the code, copy, and contracts must be maintainable by strangers. We invest in tooling, observability, and documentation as first-class deliverables.

04

Regional by default

Singapore HQ, but the work spans six time zones. We design for Bahasa, Thai, Vietnamese, Tagalog, and Mandarin from day zero — not as an afterthought.

05

Commercial curiosity

Every engineer talks to sellers. Every operator reads platform changelogs. Understanding the P&L of a TikTok Shop live stream changes how you architect the data layer.

06

Low ego, high standards

We celebrate the fix more than the feature. Post-mortems are blameless. Promotions follow impact, not tenure. The best idea wins, regardless of title.

05 / HIRING PROCESS

Five steps, two weeks target

We respect your time. Our process is designed to be rigorous, transparent, and fast.

01

Intro call (30 min)

With a hiring manager. Mutual fit: your trajectory, our challenges, the role scope, and indicative compensation band. No tricks.

02

Take-home craft exercise (2–3 hrs)

Realistic, scoped problem relevant to the role. Engineering: a small service or PR review. Design: a flow + component spec. Growth: a channel experiment design. Paid at S$200/hr for your time.

03

Technical review (60 min)

Walk through your solution with two future peers. We evaluate reasoning, trade-offs, and communication — not whether you guessed the "right" answer.

04

Values & collaboration loop (45 min)

Cross-functional panel. Scenarios on disagreement, prioritisation, and failure. We look for alignment with our operating principles.

05

Offer & reference

Verbal offer with full package breakdown (base, equity, benefits, band). Two professional references (we call, you don't coordinate). Decision within 48 hours of final interview.

Timeline illustrative. Complex roles may add a system design or live pairing session. We communicate status at every stage.

06 / CANDIDATE COMMITMENTS

What you can expect from us

Hiring is a two-way contract. We hold ourselves to these standards.

Transparent pay ranges on offer

Every offer letter includes the role's salary band, equity grant size with current valuation context, and the percentile your offer sits at. No "competitive package" vagueness.

Response within 10 working days

After each stage, you receive a yes/no/next-steps update within 10 business days. If we slip, we proactively notify you with a revised date. Ghosting is a firing offence for interviewers.

Fraud warning: ALATA never asks candidates for payment

We never request money for equipment, visa processing, background checks, or training. All official communication comes from @alata.example domains. Report impersonation to hello@alata.example.

Detailed feedback on request

If you complete the craft exercise, you may request written feedback regardless of outcome. We provide specific, actionable notes within 5 business days.

No explosive offers

Offers expire no sooner than 14 calendar days. We encourage you to compare, negotiate, and decide calmly. Pressure tactics undermine long-term trust.

Data privacy

Your application data is stored in our ATS (Greenhouse) for 12 months max, accessible only to the hiring team. Request deletion anytime at hello@alata.example. See Privacy Policy.

07 / FAQ

Common questions

Do you sponsor Employment Passes in Singapore?

Yes. For eligible roles we sponsor EP or S Pass applications and cover associated fees. Relocation support includes flight, 30-day serviced apartment, deposit assistance, and immigration legal counsel.

Can I work fully remote from another Southeast Asian country?

Yes, for most roles. You must reside in a country where we have entity or EOR coverage (currently SG, MY, ID, PH, TH, VN) and maintain ±3 hours overlap with Singapore core hours (10:00–16:00 SGT).

What does the equity grant look like?

Options granted under our ESOP with standard 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff, monthly vesting thereafter. Strike price set at latest 409A-equivalent valuation. Refresh grants at annual reviews for top contributors.

How do you handle performance reviews and progression?

Semi-annual cycles. Progression framework published internally with clear competencies per level (IC and management tracks). Promotions require demonstrated sustained impact at the next level, not just tenure.

What is the interview-to-offer ratio?

Roughly 1:5 for final-round candidates. We over-invest in top-of-funnel sourcing to keep the pipeline diverse and the bar high.

Do you hire interns or new graduates?

We run a structured 12-week internship programme (Engineering, Product, Growth) each May–August. Applications open in January. New-grad roles posted as available — typically 2–3 per year.

What does "sample listings" mean on the roles above?

The six roles shown are illustrative placeholders representing the types of problems we solve. Actual open positions change weekly. Always check careers@alata.example for current openings.

How do I know a recruiting email is genuinely from ALATA?

Official emails only from @alata.example. We never use Gmail, Outlook, or personal domains. We never ask for payment, ID documents before offer stage, or remote desktop access. Verify suspicious messages at hello@alata.example.
