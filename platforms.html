01 / TIKTOK SHOP

TikTok Shop — Content-first commerce at scale

The fastest-growing commerce surface in Southeast Asia. Discovery-driven, video-native, algorithmic distribution. Requires creative velocity, live operations, and real-time inventory sync.

01

How TikTok Shop works

TikTok Shop merges entertainment and transaction in a single feed. Products surface through short-form video, live streams, the Shop tab, and affiliate creator content. The algorithm optimises for watch-time, engagement rate, and conversion velocity — not keyword relevance. Sellers must feed the content engine daily: 3–5 shoppable videos, 2–4 live hours, and a rotating affiliate roster. Inventory, pricing, and promotions sync via API or seller centre; mismatches trigger instant penalties. The platform takes a referral fee (1–5% by category) plus payment processing (2–3%). Payouts settle T+1 to T+3 depending on market. Compliance layers include product authenticity verification, restricted goods gating, and local consumer protection mandates (e.g., Indonesia’s PMSE, Vietnam’s Decree 85).

02

What ALATA does

We operate your TikTok Shop end-to-end: content factory (scripts, shoot, edit, caption, tag), live commerce calendar (host recruitment, run-of-show, real-time offer engine), affiliate programme management (outreach, commission tiers, sample logistics, performance tracking), catalogue health (attribute completeness, GTIN validation, image compliance), ad operations (TikTok Ads Manager — Spark Ads, Video Shopping Ads, LIVE Shopping Ads), and settlement reconciliation (fee audit, VAT computation, payout forecasting). All workflows run through our control plane: creative approvals, inventory buffers, price rules, and anomaly alerts — no manual CSV uploads.

02 / SHOPEE

Shopee — Search-led mass market dominance

Shopee leads GMV across SEA with a search-first marketplace, deep promotional calendar (9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12), and the region’s most mature logistics network (Shopee Express, FBS, Seller Flex). Winning requires promotional discipline, keyword mastery, and fulfilment excellence.

01

How Shopee works

Shoppers enter via search, category browse, and platform-wide flash sales. Ranking factors: sales velocity, conversion rate, keyword relevance, seller rating, chat response time, and participation in platform campaigns (Flash Deal, Shopee Mall, Free Shipping). The promotional calendar drives 40–60% of annual GMV in major campaigns. Sellers run Shop Vouchers, Bundle Deals, Coins Cashback, and Free Shipping subsidies — each with platform co-funding rules. Fulfilment: FBS (Fulfilled by Shopee) offers prime badge and faster checkout; Seller Flex enables own-warehouse with Shopee pickup. Shopee Express handles last-mile in most markets. Referral fees 1–12% by category and mall status. Payment processing 2–3%. Settlement T+2 to T+5 post-delivery confirmation (escrow model). Cross-border (CBSC) routes via Singapore/Shenzhen hubs with LGS-equivalent sorting.

02

What ALATA does

Campaign calendar management (submission, creative, inventory allocation, budget pacing), keyword portfolio (research, listing optimisation, backend search terms, auto-suggest mining), FBS/Seller Flex orchestration (inbound planning, storage fee optimisation, removal orders), Shopee Ads operations (Search Ads, Discovery Ads, Display, KOL/affiliate), chat SLA automation (auto-reply, ticket routing, CSAT tracking), and financial reconciliation (settlement parsing, fee audit, campaign subsidy verification, VAT/withholding tax). Our control plane enforces campaign guardrails: min ROAS, max discount depth, inventory buffers — preventing oversell during flash sales.

03 / LAZADA

Lazada — Brand-first ecosystem with infrastructure depth

Lazada (Alibaba Group) combines a premium shopper base, LazMall authenticity guarantee, and the region’s most sophisticated cross-border infrastructure (LGS, LGF, Cainiao). Winning demands brand authority, PDP excellence, and cross-border fluency.

01

How Lazada works

Two parallel tracks: LazMall (brand-authorised, 100% authentic, 15-day return, dedicated support) and Marketplace (open sellers). Traffic splits: organic search (~55%), paid (Sponsored Products, Display, Brand Zone ~25%), direct/brand store (~15%), affiliate/off-site (~5%). LazMall entry requires brand authorisation, trademark registration, and quality audit. Commission: LazMall 2–8% + platform fee; Marketplace 1–15%. Lazada Global Sorting (LGS) inbound: sellers ship to Hong Kong/Korea/China hubs; Lazada handles customs, line-haul, last-mile. Lazada Global Fulfilment (LGF) stores inventory in-market for 1–2 day delivery. LazLive supplements but is not primary. Settlement bi-weekly or monthly. Strong B2B/wholesale channel (Lazada Wholesale) for bulk buyers.

02

What ALATA does

LazMall application & brand registry (trademark, authorisation letters, quality docs), Brand Store design (modules, storytelling, CRM capture), PDP optimisation (rich content, video, 3D/AR, review seeding), Sponsored Products & Display management (keyword harvesting, bid rules, dayparting), LGS/LGF orchestration (inbound forecasting, storage optimisation, removal), Lazada Affiliate & KOL programmes, CRM (Lazada CRM — coupons, follow prizes, member tiers), and reconciliation (settlement, fee audit, cross-border duty/tax). Control plane syncs inventory across LGF, own-warehouse, and 3PL — single available-to-promise view.

03

Key levers

  • LGF coverage: target 80%+ of top 20% SKUs by velocity
  • Brand Store traffic share: > 30% of brand GMV
  • Sponsored Products ROAS: illustrative target 4.0x
  • Cross-border duty-paid (DDP) adoption: 100% for CB SKUs
  • Review velocity: 50+ new reviews/month per hero SKU

Illustrative targets. LazMall brands typically see higher AOV and lower return rates.
