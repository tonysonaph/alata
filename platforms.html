Platforms / One connected operation

Different platforms. One considered way to sell.

ALATA brings the practical work of Southeast Asian commerce into one operating rhythm—from preparing your stores and content to fulfilling orders, supporting customers and reading performance.

01 / A channel portfolio, not a collection of silos

Each platform has its own rhythm. Your operation should not feel fragmented.

TikTok Shop, Shopee and Lazada each offer distinct ways for customers to discover, consider and purchase. ALATA helps you respect those differences without running three disconnected businesses.

We establish a shared commercial foundation: clear product information, consistent brand presentation, coordinated campaign planning, dependable fulfilment and a common view of what is happening. The result is less duplicated effort and more room to make thoughtful decisions for each channel.

  • Channel-specific execution — approaches shaped around how audiences browse and buy on each platform.
  • Shared standards — coherent naming, merchandising, service and reporting across the portfolio.
  • Central coordination — one accountable operating layer connecting campaigns, stock, orders, customers and finance.

02 / TikTok Shop

Make the product worth watching.

TikTok Shop brings discovery, content, creators and commerce into one place. For brands, the opportunity is not simply to place a product in a catalogue, but to create a credible reason for people to pay attention.

ALATA helps translate that potential into an operating routine. We prepare the storefront, organise the catalogue, support livestreams and creator activity, and connect the moments of attention to the work required when an order comes in.

06 / How ALATA works across platforms

A single operating layer, with room for each channel to feel distinct.

The value of a multi-platform operation comes from coordination. ALATA connects the commercial decisions made before launch with the operational work required after a customer buys.

We begin with the business, not the channel list. We consider the product, audience, target markets, fulfilment model and service standards before shaping the platform plan.

From there, the same operating principles connect the work: one source of commercial intent, clear ownership, visible dependencies and regular review of what is working.
